⬡ ARMORY
FIREARMS INVENTORY SYSTEM · v3.1.0

ARMORY is a professional-grade, offline-first firearms inventory system built as a Progressive Web App. Installable on Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS — no app store required. All data lives exclusively on your device. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or shared.

FEATURE SET
📋 INVENTORYFull spec sheets per firearm — make, model, caliber, barrel, weight, condition, value, purchase date, storage location, and photos.
🤖 AI ENGINEGroq-powered AI identifies firearms from camera photos, auto-fills specs, and writes detailed AI descriptions on demand.
🎯 RANGE LOGLog rounds per session per firearm. Live vault-wide total in the header. Click to filter only firearms with range data.
🔧 MAINTENANCEService date, 8-task checklist, and timestamped maintenance log entries per firearm.
📸 PHOTO GALLERYHero photo plus unlimited gallery images per firearm with a fullscreen lightbox viewer.
📁 DOCUMENTSAttach files (receipts, photos, PDFs) per firearm. Generate Bill of Sale, ATF 4473 checklist, and insurance reports on demand.
💾 FULL BACKUPOne JSON file backs up everything including your API key and notes. Full restore on any device in seconds.
📊 VAULT STATSLive header: firearm count, total estimated value, and total rounds fired across all firearms.

THE STORY OF ARMORY
FROM ANXIETY TO AUTOMATION
🗂 THE EXCEL PLATEAU

As a long-time firearm collector and DIY enthusiast, I hit a wall. My collection had grown into a serious investment, but my tracking system was frozen in time — a half-finished spreadsheet that was tedious to maintain and offered zero peace of mind.

Every time I added a new piece, the excitement was immediately replaced by inventory anxiety: manual data entry, missing specs, and a nagging question I couldn't shake — "If something happened today, would my insurance paperwork actually be ready?"

⚡ THE SPARK

I decided to build the tool I couldn't find — one that treated a collection like a high-tech vault rather than a grocery list. The goal was simple: bridge the gap between the physical object in my hand and a complete, always-ready digital record in my pocket.

🔧 THE EVOLUTION

What started as an early development project became a platform built on two principles: utility and privacy. I integrated computer vision and AI to eliminate the friction of data entry entirely. Instead of typing "Matte Black Cerakote" or "5.56 NATO," the app sees the firearm, reads the markings, and pulls the specs automatically.

Over time, the intelligence layer deepened — connecting to large language models via Groq to generate rich AI descriptions and manufacturer histories, turning a serial number into a story worth reading.

🎯 WHAT IT BECAME
📸

🤖
The Intelligence Layer
Groq AI provides deep-dive descriptions and brand history, turning hardware into documented history.

Insurance-Ready Exports

🔒

THE RESULT

ARMORY turned a source of stress into a source of pride. Built by a hobbyist, for hobbyists — combining the technical rigor of a developer with the passion of a collector. Your vault deserves better than a spreadsheet.
